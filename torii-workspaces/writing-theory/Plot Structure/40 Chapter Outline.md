# 40 Chapter Outline

This document summarizes the 40 chapter plot structure for stories as discussed in ["The Only Plot Structure You Will Ever Need"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vAbMAzhFiGc) by [TheNerdyNovelist](https://www.youtube.com/@TheNerdyNovelist).

- [Act 1](./40%20Chapter%20Outline/Act%201.md) - **Introduce the protagonist and ordinary world** they live in, their goals, flaws and the inciting incident that makes them leave their ordinary world.
- [Act 2A](./40%20Chapter%20Outline/Act%202A.md) - First half of the middle section, showing a positive rising action as the **protagonist enters new world**, learns, grows and has more success than failure. 
- [Act 2B](./40%20Chapter%20Outline/Act%202B.md) - Second half of the middle section, reversal of 2A with negative rising action as the **protagonist struggles and things start to fall apart**.
- [Act 3](./40%20Chapter%20Outline/Act%203.md) - Climax of the story where the **protagonist confonts their inner flaw** and overcomes it, allowing them to defeat the antagonist "despite all odds"
